FAQ

Straight answers.

What is Gachiz?

A market for collectibles. Instead of pulling a card and hoping, you take a long or short position on an index of Pokémon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh! or sports card sales.

Is Gachiz affiliated with these card games?

No. Gachiz is an independent project. Pokémon, One Piece and Yu-Gi-Oh! are trademarks of their owners: their names only say which cards an index follows.

Is trading live?

No. This site is a preview. No order is sent and no funds are taken. Connecting a wallet only reads your address.

Which chain is it on?

Robinhood Chain, an EVM network (chain id 4663).

Is there a token?

A GACHIZ token is planned. It has no contract address yet: any address you see elsewhere is not ours. Once it exists, it is shown in the top bar and the footer, with a button to copy it.
